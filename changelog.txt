Version: 1.1.0
Date: 23. 10. 2026
  Factorio 2.1:
    - Ported to Factorio 2.1 (requires base and Space Age 2.1).
    - Factorio 2.1 changed the scrap recycling recipe to one shared roll per scrap (at most one item, "shared_probability") and removed the product key "probability".
    - While all chances add up to 100% or less, the outputs get consecutive shared ranges like vanilla; the defaults give exactly the vanilla recipe.
    - Above 100% in total every output is rolled independently ("independent_probability"), as in 2.0.
    - The chance of each output is always exactly its setting. The log line names the roll mode.
  Info:
    - Added the MIT LICENSE file.
